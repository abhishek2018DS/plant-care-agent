# My agent: plant-care-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant owners care for their indoor greenery with a catalog of plants.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Remembers plant care history and watering schedules
- Tools: Looks up plant care guides and weather conditions
- Catalog/UI: Catalog of indoor plants to render as cards
- Image gen: Generates plant health progress visuals
- Sandbox: Computes custom fertilizer ratios

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI, image gen, sandbox
First eval question: What is the watering schedule for a Monstera Deliciosa?
